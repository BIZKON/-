## 2024-05-23 - [Icon Button Accessibility]
**Learning:** Text-based "x" buttons are often inaccessible and visually jarring. Users expect icon buttons with clear hit targets.
**Action:** Replace text-based "close" or "delete" characters with Semantic Icons (like Trash2) wrapped in a button with `aria-label`, explicit `type='button'`, and proper focus/hover states.
